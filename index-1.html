Sea of Fates: Fate hunt

How to play
  • Move: drag on the map, or use WASD / arrow keys. You are the blue circle.
  • One bot is your target and a different bot is secretly hunting you. All bots look alike.
  • Attack (E): get close to your target and strike. It is optional. After a kill, take a new target (H) or keep exploring.
  • Ask the Saint (F): shows how far away your hunter is, with no direction. It can be used every 30 seconds.
  • Accuse (G): type the name of your hunter. If you are right they fall. If you are wrong, you fall.
  • If your hunter touches you, you lose. Be the last one standing to win.
  • Pirate: plunder gold from others (T). Navy: sees pirates and hunts them. Celestial: when you are attacked, someone else dies instead.
  • Your name, faction, gold, and kills are saved in this browser.
