
# Sea of Fates (Prototype)

An anime-inspired open-world pirate RPG concept by Naved "Lone" Ghani.
This is a small playable prototype of its core hook, the Fate hunt.

## Play it
https://ghaninaved786-code.github.io/Sea-of-fates/
## The idea
Every player is assigned one target and is hunted by someone else, and you never know who. Hunting is optional. Information is your defense.

## How to play
- Move: drag on the map, or use WASD / arrow keys.
- One bot is your target. A different bot is secretly hunting you.
- Attack (E): get close to your target and strike.
- Ask the Saint (F): shows how far your hunter is, with no direction.
- Accuse (G): type your hunter's name. Right: they fall. Wrong: you fall.
- If your hunter touches you, you lose. Be the last one standing to win.

## Factions
- Pirate: plunder gold from others (T).
- Navy: sees pirates and hunts them.
- Celestial: when you are attacked, someone else dies instead.

## Limits of this version
- Solo only: 5 bots fill the sea. There is no live multiplayer yet.
- Progress (name, faction, gold, kills) is saved in your own browser only.
- Names and numbers are placeholders and will change in the full game.
